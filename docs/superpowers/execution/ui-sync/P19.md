## Source and design coverage

## Motion and interaction

Applied `gsap-react` and `gsap-scrolltrigger` skill guidance using the installed GSAP package. Animations are scoped to Home refs, use matchMedia and revert on unmount. No CDN script, injected HTML, global page selector, fabricated network response or delayed chat simulator was copied.

The desktop story pins for the source 3000px range and switches all six views as the reader scrolls. Its six native controls also work with keyboard and expose the selected step/controlled panel. Pinning is disabled at tablet/mobile widths, on short screens and under reduced motion. Inactive panels are hidden accessibly; reduced motion retains readable content and manual step selection. The header follows the section under it, with appropriate dark/light contrast and cleanup when navigating away. Bars retain their source values in reduced motion; reveal/progress effects are decorative.

## Intentional content/contract differences

The reference contains illustrative personal scores, salary/market claims, a 60-question Likert sample and a scripted chat response. The public Home makes example-rich regions explicitly illustrative, formats ranking examples on a /100 scale, and replaces salary/market numbers with source/catalog guidance. It does not claim to read the visitor's result, skills, progress or recommendation. The small assessment demonstration uses Có/Không, keeps its selection local and sends no request. Actual questionnaire size and scoring remain owned by the real assessment flow.

Advisor presentation is static capability/context copy; each suggested question opens the actual guarded Advisor route and never appends a fabricated reply. Removed the fixed-name greeting, always-available service claim and unverifiable validation wording. Other examples remain labelled design illustrations. No backend, endpoint, dependency, lockfile, score, session or mutation-replay changes.
